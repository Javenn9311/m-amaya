    Common questions

    Before you visit

    When will M Amaya be completed?

    Expected completion is 2030.

    Is it freehold?

    Yes. M Amaya sits on 4.8 acres of freehold land in Southbay, Batu Maung, developed by Vienna View Development Sdn Bhd, a wholly owned subsidiary of Mah Sing Group.

    What is the maintenance fee?

    RM0.33 per sq ft, including the sinking fund, as per the approved memo.

    How high are the ceilings?

    Floor-to-floor height is 3m, with up to 2,850mm clear height to the soffit.

    How many lifts are there?

    Each block has 3 passenger lifts and 1 fire lift. Estimated waiting time is about 24 seconds.

    What security is provided?

    CCTV on the perimeter and common areas, a guardhouse, licence-plate recognition for residents' cars, and access cards for the lobby and lifts.
